Shannon Source Models

A live classroom demo of the random-letter, frequency, digram, trigram, and word-level language sources from Shannon's 1948 paper. Each generated symbol exposes the conditional distribution used to pick it, then shows how quickly the table of possible contexts grows. Source model definitions follow A Mathematical Theory of Communication.
